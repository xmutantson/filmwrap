How to use

1. Add your films, ISO, dates & quantities above.
2. Tune strip length so it wraps your canister about 2-3 times.
3. Hit Print sheet. Match the paper size, set Margins: None and Scale: 100%, and turn on "Print backgrounds". Measure a test strip before printing a full batch; printer settings can change the size.
4. Cut along the lines, write the event/date later, wrap around the film & tuck into the tube.
