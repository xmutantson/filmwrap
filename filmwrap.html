How to use

1. Add your films, ISO, dates & quantities above.
2. Tune strip length so it wraps your canister about 2-3 times.
3. Hit Print sheet. In the dialog set Margins: None and turn on "Print backgrounds" so the color bars print. Scale can be 100% or Fit to page width; both print at the right size.
4. Cut along the lines, write the event/date later, wrap around the film & tuck into the tube.
